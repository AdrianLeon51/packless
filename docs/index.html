FAQ

Good questions.

How much does it cost?

We'll share prices before launch. People on the waitlist hear first. Joining the waitlist is free.

How do you know my size?

You tell us your sizes and how you like things to fit. You do it once. We pick from there.

What if something doesn't fit?

Tell us. We'll swap it while you're there.

Do I have to give everything back?

No. Return what you borrowed before you fly home. Keep anything that earned a spot in your bag.

A short list of secondhand shops in each city. We choose them for condition, size range and season.
