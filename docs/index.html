FAQ

Good questions.

Is this rental or do I buy the clothes?

It's rental. You wear the pieces on your trip and hand them back. If you love something, you can buy it. That's optional.

Can I choose my own outfits?

Yes. Browse what's in stock in your city and pick. Or set your preferences and we'll choose for you.

What if I don't like it when I see it?

You see real photos and approve everything before you fly. If it still isn't you, swap it in store.

Who owns the clothes?

Local stores in each city. They list their pieces on Packless. We don't keep our own stock. Every store meets our standards: pieces are condition checked, professionally cleaned, and measured so sizes are documented properly.

How much does it cost?

We'll share prices before launch. People on the waitlist hear first. Joining the waitlist is free.

How do you know my size?

You save your sizes once. Every piece has measured, documented sizing, so you can check the fit before you pick it. And you can try pieces on when you collect them.

What if something doesn't fit?

Tell us. We'll swap it while you're there.

What if the weather changes or a plan comes up?

Add or swap pieces while you're there. That's what Packless is for.

Which cities can I use it in?

Lisbon opens first. Barcelona, Paris and Amsterdam follow. Cities open in stages.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

You get one email when your city opens. Nothing to pay today. No spam.
